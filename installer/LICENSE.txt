1. WHAT IT IS
   Hunt Tracker is a free, unofficial companion window for Hunt: Showdown 1896.
   It is not made, endorsed or supported by Crytek, Steam or Valve.

2. WHAT IT DOES ON YOUR PC
   - It reads Hunt's own log file (Game.log) to know when you are in a match.
   - While Hunt is showing, it takes screenshots of certain screens
     (loading cards, death screens, post-match summary, Team Details, your
     Statistics page and the map timer) and reads the text on them with
     Windows' built-in text recognition.
   - It checks whether the Tab key is held down during a hunt, to read the
     map's timer, and listens for the End key, which opens the overlay.
   - Only while the overlay is open, it keeps your mouse clicks, scroll wheel
     and Esc on Hunt's screen from reaching the game (a standard Windows
     keyboard/mouse hook), so clicking the overlay doesn't click in the game.
   - It saves what it reads (stats, player names, MMR stars, and the text of
     those screens; not the images) in your user folder:
     %LOCALAPPDATA%\HuntTracker
   - It never changes, injects into, or reads the memory of the game.
   - Internet: it checks GitHub for updates when it opens. If you choose to
     share on the leaderboard (it asks first, and you can stop any time),
     your player name and Career numbers (kills, deaths, K/D, bounty...) are
     uploaded to the Hunt Tracker leaderboard.
   - Diagnostics: to find and fix problems, it sends the developer a
     diagnostics report about once an hour: the tracker's version and setup
     (Windows version, screen sizes, Hunt's display mode, overlay or window),
     your player name and Career stats (kills, deaths, K/D, prestige, rank,
     weapon and hunter kills, MMR, hunts and hours tracked), how its screen
     reading went over the last week (counts only), its error logs, and the
     text it recently read from Hunt's screens.
     That text includes the names of other players shown on your loading
     cards, squad page and death screens. Reports can only be read by the
     developer. It asks once when it first starts; you can switch it off any
     time by right-clicking the tracker's icon by the clock ("Send
     diagnostics"). Nothing else is sent.

3. OPTIONAL AUTO-START
   If you choose it during setup, a small helper starts with Windows and
   opens the tracker whenever Hunt starts. You can turn this off by
   uninstalling, or by deleting "Hunt Tracker (opens with Hunt)" from your
   Startup folder (Win+R, then type shell:startup).

5. UNINSTALLING
   You can uninstall it any time from Windows Settings > Apps. You will be
   asked whether to also delete your saved stats.
